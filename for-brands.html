What we do

Five commitments to every brand partner.

MAP Pricing Respect

We follow your Minimum Advertised Price policy on every channel where we list your products. We do not race to the bottom on price, and we price to protect the value of your brand.

If your MAP changes, tell us once and we update every listing.

Accurate Listings

Titles, images, bullet points, and descriptions should match what you approve. We work from your brand assets and correct listing errors when we find them, so shoppers see the same product story everywhere.

Reliable Inventory

We plan purchases around real sell-through so your products stay in stock without flooding the market. Steady, predictable reorders give you a clearer picture of demand.

Authentic Products

Every unit we sell comes directly from you or from a distributor you authorize. We keep invoices on file for every purchase, so the chain of custody is documented.

Marketplace Monitoring

We watch your listings for sellers offering your products below MAP, with poor condition, or through unclear sourcing. When we spot one, we report it to you with the details you need to act.

How we help you grow

A partner that adds to your brand.

We don’t just list your products and wait. We work with you to figure out what we can contribute that helps your brand most.

Marketplace advertising

We plan and run sponsored ads and promotions for your products, within your pricing policy and brand guidelines, to drive visibility and sales.

Channel selection

We look at where your products fit best and recommend which online marketplaces and stores to sell through, so you grow in the right places.

Listing and content improvements

We find gaps in titles, images, and product details and suggest improvements that help shoppers understand and choose your products.

Shared priorities

We agree on goals with you up front and adjust as we learn what works, so our effort goes where it matters most for your brand.

The partnership

From first call to steady reorders.

Most partnerships follow the same path. We keep it simple and move at a pace that works for your team.

  1. Intro conversation

    We learn about your brand, your goals, your current channels, and how you want your products represented online.

  2. Account setup

    We complete your wholesale application and provide our business and resale documentation.

  3. Opening order

    We place a starting order sized to test demand and build listings that meet your standards.

  4. Growth plan

    Together we decide where we contribute: advertising, which channels to sell through, and how to present your products.

  5. Listing and selling

    We manage listings, pricing within your policy, inventory, advertising, and customer experience.

  6. Regular check-ins

    We share sell-through, reorder plans, and anything we see on your listings.

What to expect

What you can count on from us.

We would rather earn a long relationship than a quick sale. These are the standards we hold ourselves to with every brand.

  • A single point of contact who owns the relationship
  • Payment on the terms we agree to, every time
  • Strict adherence to your MAP and brand guidelines
  • Documented, authorized sourcing for every unit
  • Prompt notice when we see a problem on your listings
