Maple Valley Supply is a Texas-based ecommerce retailer that partners with consumer brands to sell their products online.

Our story

Maple Valley Supply was founded to build a retail business that brands can trust with their products and their reputation.

Many online sellers treat brands as a source of cheap inventory. We take the opposite view. A brand that sees its pricing respected, its listings handled with care, and its invoices paid on time is a brand that wants to keep working together.

That is the whole model. Buy from authorized sources, follow the brand’s rules, communicate clearly, and pay on time.

We also treat every brand as a partner, not a supplier. We work with each brand to figure out where we can contribute the most, whether that is marketplace advertising, deciding which online channels to sell through, or improving how products are presented.
